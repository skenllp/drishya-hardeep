## ✅ All Form Data is Captured!

Every time someone submits the RSVP form, **ALL** of this information is saved to your Google Sheet:

## 🔍 Field Details

### 1. **Timestamp** (Column A)
- 📅 Automatically generated by the script
- 🕐 Shows date and time of submission
- 📍 Format: `dd/mm/yyyy hh:mm:ss`
- Example: `16/11/2026 14:30:25`

### 2. **Name** (Column B)
- 👤 Guest's full name from the form
- ✅ Required field
- Example: `Rajesh Kumar`

### 3. **Phone** (Column C)
- 📱 Phone number with country code
- ✅ Required field
- Example: `+91 9876543210`

### 4. **Guests** (Column D)
- 👥 Number of people attending
- 🔢 Numeric value (minimum 1)
- Example: `3`

### 5. **Attending** (Column E)
- ✅ Will they attend?
- 💚 `yes` = "COUNT ME IN" button clicked
- 💙 `no` = "SENDING MY LOVE" button clicked
- Example: `yes` or `no`

### 6. **Message** (Column F)
- 💬 Personal message/blessings for the couple
- 📝 Optional field (can be blank)
- 📏 No length limit
- Example: `Looking forward to celebrating with you! Congratulations on your special day! 🎉💑`
